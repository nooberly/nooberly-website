Child Safety Standards

Published September 11, 2026

Nooberly, Inc. ("Nooberly", "we", "us") runs the Nooberly app. These are our published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). They apply to every member, every post, every message and every video on Nooberly, everywhere in the world.

1. Zero tolerance

Nooberly prohibits any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits, grooms, endangers or harms a child, or that solicits, shares, describes or links to such content. This includes sexual or suggestive material involving minors, requests for it, and attempts to contact a minor for a sexual purpose. There are no exceptions and no warnings: an account that does any of this is terminated.

2. Who can be on Nooberly

  • Nobody under 13 may hold an account. Signup asks for a date of birth, and the face verification at signup includes an automated age estimate; accounts that fail either check are refused.
  • Members aged 13 to 17 are placed in a junior tier that is kept apart from adult members for meeting and messaging features.
  • Every account is tied to one verified human face. One person, one account. A terminated person cannot come back under another email.

3. How to report

  • In the app: every profile, post, message and review carries a flag. A flag goes straight to our founder-run review queue and is never shown publicly.
  • By email: team@nooberly.com. Put "child safety" in the subject and we treat it as an emergency.
  • Anyone can report, member or not. Reports can be anonymous.

4. What we do when we receive a report

  • We review CSAE reports within 24 hours, ahead of everything else in the queue.
  • Confirmed CSAM is removed immediately, the account is terminated, and the person's verified face record is kept so they cannot return.
  • We report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in the United States and to the relevant law enforcement authority in the reporter's country, and we preserve the evidence for them as required by law.
  • We cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement anywhere Nooberly operates.

5. Prevention

  • Videos posted to the feed and used as profile videos are screened by automated content review before they are shown to other members.
  • Messages and reviews can be flagged by any member at any time, and our AI assistant captures reported content for the review queue.
  • Exact locations are never shown to other members; neighbours see a distance, never a door.

6. Compliance

Nooberly complies with the child safety laws of every country in which the app is available, including the United States federal reporting duties for providers, and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and Apple's App Review Guidelines on objectionable content.

7. Designated contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety, able to speak about our CSAM prevention practices and compliance, is reachable at team@nooberly.com.
