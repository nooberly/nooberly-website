4. Star Power

Star Power ("SP") is the number that shows how much trust you have earned. It is tied to your face, not your email.

(a) Star Power is earned one way: through reviews written after real connections. Two small exceptions: a one-time welcome grant of 33 SP, given once per face, ever; and a 3 SP gift to the member who invited you, paid once, inside the review they write after meeting you. Nothing else mints it — not badges, not logging in.
(b) You spend Star Power on actions inside the App — for example to post, to apply to a post, to open a chat, to use Meet Now, to have the AI draft a review, and to boost a review you write.
(c) You can send Star Power to another member, and ask a contact for some. That is a gift between the two of you; we take nothing from it.
(d) Star Power has no cash value. It cannot be bought, sold, or cashed out, and we will never sell it to you.
(e) Your Star Power balance and its full history survive account deletion and come back with your face.
(f) We may adjust Star Power balances to undo fraud or abuse.

5. Sooper Power

Sooper Power is a second number. Tips and Supporter subscriptions mint Sooper Power: each week, what came in through them (after store fees and any declared server costs) becomes that week's pot, and the whole pot is shared among the members who earned the most Star Power that week. Payday is Friday. The books are open, to the dollar, at nooberly.com/ledger.

Please read this part twice:

(a) Sooper Power is a number the Kingdom mints, not money. It is not cash, not a share of Nooberly, and not a payout in dollars. It has no cash value and cannot be bought, sold, or cashed out.
(b) We do not promise that you will ever receive Sooper Power, or any particular amount of it. Do not join Nooberly expecting to earn money.
(c) You can send Sooper Power to another member, the same way as Star Power.
(d) Sooper Power is a bridge, not a destination. Nooberly's aim is one number — Star Power — and we may change or retire Sooper Power in the future. What you already hold stays yours.
(e) Like Star Power, it survives account deletion and comes back with your face.

(b) A review is a rating from 1 to 5 stars plus your words or a short video. Our AI reads it, together with the chat that led to the meeting, to score the Star Power it earns. Reviews are revealed once both of you have written one, or when the review window closes.
(c) Reviews are permanent. A review written about you belongs to the community, not to you. You cannot edit it, hide it, or have it removed, and you may not demand that we delete it.
(d) Behaviour flags our AI draws from low-star reviews are kept as part of your safety record, even if you delete your account.
(e) If you delete your account, reviews you wrote stay, shown as written by a "Deleted Noober". If you come back, your name returns to them.

(g) You will not post reviews that are knowingly false, defamatory, or harassing. We may remove reviews that are fraudulent or abusive.

10. Location

Distance is the product, so Nooberly needs to know where you are. The App asks for location permission while you are using it. While the App is open, we read your location and store it with your profile at the precision you choose.

You control how precisely others see you, and you can change it any time in Settings:

(a) Exact — we store your real position and the street address it maps to; others see your real distance (for example, "0.4 mi").
(b) Approximate — the default. We store a position rounded to about a kilometre and no street address, and work out only your neighbourhood and city from it; others see a rounded distance ("within 3 mi").
(c) Hidden — we store no location at all, and choosing it erases the location we had.

Availability is a separate switch. When you are available, people nearby can see your distance and your pin, find you in Nearby, and ping you about posts. When you are off, your distance and pin stop showing, post pings stop, and you show as offline — but we still store your location unless you choose Hidden, you can still turn up in searches by distance, and Meet Now may still reach you if you were active in the last hour. A post you create carries a meeting place, and that place is public because it is where people will meet you. Meet Now uses your live location to find people up to 33 miles away.
