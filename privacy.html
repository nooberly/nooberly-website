Privacy Policy

Last modified September 5, 2026

Nooberly, Inc. ("Nooberly", "we", "us") runs the Nooberly app and the website at nooberly.com. This page explains what we collect about you, why, who else handles it, what other people can see, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. We wrote it in plain English on purpose. If anything is unclear, write to team@nooberly.com.

1. The short version

  • Nooberly is for adults. You must be 18 or older to have an account.
  • Your face is your identity here. You scan it once. One person gets one account. The face record stays even if you leave — that is how nobody can shed a bad reputation by signing up again.
  • You choose how precisely people see where you are: exact, approximate (the default), or hidden.
  • We do not sell your data. There are no ads and no advertising code in the app.
  • Other members see the profile you build, the reviews you have received and your Star Power. Guests who have not joined see less.
  • You can delete your account in the app any time. It waits 33 days in case you change your mind, then it goes — except the safety records listed in Section 10.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the app — your account, profile, posts, chats, calls, reviews and calendar.
  • To verify who you are — one face, one account; to stop fraud, and to stop people who were removed from coming back under a new name.
  • To show you people and posts nearby, and to show you to them, at the precision you chose.
  • To keep people safe — automated checks on videos, photos and post titles; handling reports; enforcing blocks.
  • To run Nooberly's AI — transcribe your video, fill in your profile, answer questions, draft a review when you ask, suggest meeting times, and let your own AI answer other members.
  • To score reviews and work out Star Power, badges and Sooper Power.
  • To send notifications you have turned on.
  • To confirm purchases made through Apple or Google.
  • To meet our legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

4. Your face

Before your first scan we show you a consent screen that says, in short: your face becomes your key; one human, one account, ever; your face record stays even if you leave; and going available shares your location at the precision you pick. When you tap "I agree — start the scan", we record the time of that agreement on your account.

Here is what happens with the scan:

  • It is a 3D liveness scan. It confirms there is a live person in front of the camera, not a photo or a video.
  • It is matched against every face record we hold. If your face already owns an active account, we ask you to sign in to that one. If it belongs to an account that was deleted, we recognise you and offer to restore your history (Section 10). Only a genuinely new face gets the one-time 33 Star Power welcome.
  • It is stored on a verification server we control, running FaceTec's software, under your account ID. Your phone never talks to that server directly — only our own servers do. Accounts created before we moved to FaceTec also have a face vector stored with AWS Rekognition.
  • Your profile video must show the same person as the scan. When you set a new profile photo, we compare it with your verified face.
  • We may ask you to scan again when you sign in on a new phone, or from time to time.
  • Age estimates. Our verification tools may estimate an age range from your face. We use that only to spot accounts that look under 18 for review. It never changes your age on its own.
  • It is never sold, and never shared with anyone for their own purposes.
  • It stays after you delete your account. This is deliberate and you should know it before you scan. Section 10 explains why and the legal basis.

5. Your location

The app asks for location permission while you are using it (not in the background). When you open the app, and each time you come back to it, we read your position and save it to your profile at the precision you have chosen. Location is what makes Nooberly work — it is how you find people near you and they find you.

You control how precise that is. Three settings, in your profile's Settings and on the Available switch:

  • Exact — we store your real position and the street address your phone derives from it. Others see an exact distance, like "0.4 mi".
  • Approximate — the default. We store a position rounded to about a kilometre and no street address; we work out only your neighbourhood and city from it. Others see a distance band, like "within 3 mi", never tighter than one mile.
  • Hidden — we store no position at all. Others see nothing.

The Available switch is a separate control. When it is on, you have a pin on the map, you appear in Nearby, and people can ping you about posts near you. When it is off, your pin comes off the map, post pings stop, and you show as offline — but your distance setting above still applies. You cannot be Available and Hidden at the same time; being available needs a distance.

Other members see your area — a town or neighbourhood — not your street. Posts carry their own meeting spot, which is public because it is the point of the post. We also keep the rough city of the network you sign in from, so you can tell your own sessions apart. Your location is never shared with anyone for advertising.

6. Nooberly's AI

Nooberly has one AI, and it shows up in four places: a limited guest version before you join, the concierge you can ask anything, your own AI that you train, and other members' AIs that you can talk to. It reads member data only through our own servers, never straight from the database.

Your own AI answers other verified members from the answers you typed for it, your profile, your posts, your rating and local standing, and a line from your best review — all things a member can already see on your profile. About the visitor it is talking to, it reads only their own public profile and the categories their reviews were filed under — the topic of a meeting, never what someone wrote about them. If a visitor asks it to pass a message to you, it does so as your AI, and the visitor is asked to confirm first.

Automated processing you should know about:

  • Your profile video, review videos, voice notes and chat clips are transcribed by OpenAI's Whisper.
  • Your profile video transcript is used to draft your profile text and detect your language.
  • Videos, photos and post titles are checked automatically for content that is not allowed here. Photos are checked by AWS Rekognition and by an OpenAI vision model.
  • Reviews are read by our AI to score them for Star Power and to note behaviour flags. Those flags are safety records.
  • When you ask the AI to draft a review, it reads your chat with that person to write it.
  • When a chat talks about a time to meet, an automated helper reads the recent messages in that chat to suggest a calendar time.
  • Photos you add to your AI are described by an OpenAI vision model so it can talk about them.
  • When you talk to the AI by voice, your speech is transcribed and answered by OpenAI's real-time model. If you choose to give your AI your own voice — a separate opt-in, off by default — the sample you record goes to ElevenLabs to build the voice, and we note your consent on your account.

Your conversations with the AI are saved to your account. Nobody at Nooberly reads your chats as a matter of routine. A person may read a chat only when someone reports it, or when the law requires it.

All of our language and vision models come from OpenAI and are used under OpenAI's API terms — your data is not used to train their models.

8. What other people can see

Other members

Members are people who have verified their face. A member who opens your profile can see:

  • Your display name, username, age, gender, relationship status, profession, languages, what you offer and what you are looking for, your status line, and your area (not your street).
  • Your profile photo, photos, and profile video.
  • Whether you are available, when you were last active, and your distance at the precision you chose.
  • Your posts.
  • Every review you have received, and the reviews you have written.
  • Your Star Power, your badges, and your Supporter mark if you have one.
  • The answers you gave your AI, through your AI.
  • Your contacts — the people you have exchanged reviews with — and which of them they also know.

Your face, your choice. At signup you can choose to show your face only to members of your own gender. Members outside that group still see you as a person — your name and profile — but not your face or photos, unless you started a chat with them or the two of you have completed a connection.

Your QR code is a link to your profile. Anyone who scans it opens your profile in the app. If someone joins Nooberly by scanning your code, we note that you invited them.

Reports. If someone reports a chat, a person on our side may read that chat thread to decide what to do.

Guests

People who have not joined can browse the feed, watch profile videos, and read the reviews members have been given. They see your display name, age, gender, relationship status, profession, city, languages, what you offer and are looking for, your status line, whether you are available and when you were last active, your review and badge counts, your Supporter mark, and your posts and their meeting spots. They do not see your birthday, your street or neighbourhood, your distance or position, your Star Power balance, or your contacts. Your face setting applies to guests too. Nobody is told a guest looked, and a guest cannot contact you.

The public ledger

Each week we publish the Kingdom's money in and out at nooberly.com/ledger. It shows totals only. It never shows member names or individual amounts.

10. Deleting your account — and what stays

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. You can also email team@nooberly.com and we will start it for you.

Deletion has a 33-day waiting period. During that time your profile is taken out of feeds and search. If you sign back in before the 33 days are up, that cancels the deletion — coming back is the cancellation. The 33 days count from the moment you confirm.

When the 33 days pass, we delete your profile, your posts, your calendar, your sessions and your sign-in account.

What stays, and why. Nooberly only works if a reputation cannot be shed by starting over. So these are kept after deletion, attached to your face record rather than to a name:

  • The face record itself.
  • The raw clips of your profile video, as identity evidence.
  • Reviews you were given and reviews you wrote, badges, and behaviour flags.
  • Your Star Power and Sooper Power ledgers.
  • Your chats and messages.
  • Reports, blocks and security incidents.
  • Purchase receipts.
  • The profile snapshot described in Section 9.

If you ever come back, we recognise your face, show you what was kept, and restore it to your new account. You never get a second 33 Star Power welcome.

Legal basis. We keep these records under our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe — stopping fraud, ban evasion and the erasing of reviews other people wrote in good faith (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). For the face record we also rely on the explicit consent you gave on the consent screen before your first scan (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)). You can object to this retention (Section 11) and we will weigh your objection against those interests and reply.

11. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct — fix something that is wrong. Most of your profile you can edit in the app yourself. Your date of birth and gender are locked after signup; email us if one is wrong.
  • Delete — remove your account (Section 10), subject to the safety records kept there.
  • Port — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Object — object to processing we base on legitimate interest, including the retention in Section 10.
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on your consent (the face scan, location permission, the AI voice). Withdrawing does not undo processing that already happened, and the face record is kept as described in Section 10.

Email team@nooberly.com. We reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm you are the account holder first.

If you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland

Our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (running the app and your account); your consent (the face scan, location permission, and giving your AI your voice); our legitimate interests (safety, fraud prevention, and the records kept after deletion); and legal obligation. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are in California

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We collect the categories listed in Section 2, including sensitive personal information — your face scan and your precise location — which we use only for the purposes in this policy. You have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be discriminated against for using those rights. California's "Shine the Light" law (Civil Code Section 1798.83) lets you ask about any disclosure of personal information to third parties for their direct marketing; we make none. To make any of these requests, email team@nooberly.com.

12. Adults only

Nooberly is for people aged 18 and over. You enter your date of birth at signup, the app refuses anyone under 18, and our database refuses too. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will close it and delete the personal data we collected from them. If you believe that has happened, tell us at team@nooberly.com.

13. Security

  • Everything between the app and our servers travels over encrypted connections (TLS).
  • The app never talks to our cloud services directly — every call goes through our own servers.
  • Our face verification server sits on a private network that only our own servers can reach.
  • Chat photos, voice notes and clips live in private storage and are handed out with short-lived signed links.
  • Our database uses row-level security policies, and the app has no direct table access to columns like your birthday, address and balances.
  • Our storage providers encrypt data at rest.
  • Only one device can be signed in at a time. Signing in on a new phone can trigger a fresh face scan.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise absolute security. If you think your account has been touched by someone else, email us straight away.

14. Where your data lives

Our servers and storage are in the United States, and the providers in Section 7 process data there and in other countries where they operate. If you use Nooberly from outside the United States, your data is transferred there. Where the law requires it, we rely on approved safeguards for those transfers, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

15. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version. Using Nooberly after a change means you accept the updated policy.
