Kind Power — Official Rules

Last updated September 24, 2026

Apple Inc. is not a sponsor of this program and is not involved in it in any way. Google LLC is not a sponsor of this program and is not involved in it in any way.

1. What Kind Power is

  • Kind Power (KP) is a thank-you unit inside the Nooberly app. Each week a pot of Kind Power is shared among the members who earned the most Star Power from reviews that week.
  • Kind Power has no cash value. It cannot be cashed out, withdrawn or swapped for money. Nooberly never buys it back.
  • Kind Power cannot be bought. You can send it to another member inside the app.
  • It exists only inside Nooberly. It is not a cryptocurrency and is not on any blockchain.
  • Some older Nooberly pages call it Sooper Power (SP*). It is the same thing.

2. Who can take part

  • Every Nooberly member. Members are 18 or over.
  • One person, one account. Extra or duplicate accounts get nothing.
  • Void where prohibited by law.

3. No purchase necessary

  • You never need to buy anything to get Kind Power. Buying something does not help you get it.
  • Tips and the Supporter badge are what fill the pot. They are never counted when members are ranked. A member who has never paid is ranked exactly like one who has.

4. How a week works

  • A week runs from Friday 00:00 UTC to the next Friday 00:00 UTC.
  • The pot is shared later that same Friday.
  • It goes to the top 12% of members who earned Star Power from reviews that week, rounded up, and to at least 3 people when 3 or more earned any.
  • Each person's share follows how much of that Star Power they earned, next to the others in the top group.
  • Shares are counted to 0.01 KP and add up to the whole pot.
  • If two people tie at the cut-off line, the one who has gone longest without a share comes first.
  • If nobody earns Star Power from reviews in a week, no Kind Power is made that week.

5. Which Star Power counts

  • Star Power from reviews of real meetings: for a review you receive, for a review you write, and the boost a review's writer adds to it.
  • The Kingdom's gift when two people it introduced meet for the first time and both write a review.
  • Nothing else counts. Not Star Power another member sends you, and not the welcome gift.
  • Star Power cannot be bought.

6. Where the pot comes from

  • Kind Power is not a share of money. It is made new each week.
  • Each week we add up the tips and Supporter payments that are confirmed. We take off the store's fee, counted as 30%. A card payment on the web has the card company's fee taken off instead. Nothing else is taken off. The Kind Power made is in proportion to what is left.
  • A payment counts in the first weekly pot after it is confirmed.
  • No money is ever paid out, split or sent to any member. Members get Kind Power and nothing else. Payments stay with Nooberly. No member can claim them.

7. Not a lottery

No draw and no random pick decide who gets Kind Power. The Star Power a member earned from reviews that week decides who gets it and how much. A tie at the cut-off line is settled as in section 4.

8. Fair play

We may hold back a share, undo one, or take a member out of the weekly pot if we have good reason to suspect fraud, duplicate accounts, fake reviews, members working together to cheat, or any other trick to move the ranking. Our decision is final.

9. Between members

You can send Kind Power to another member in the app. Buying or selling Kind Power for money breaks our Terms of Use. Nooberly is not part of any deal two members make, and gives no refunds or help with such deals. Whoever holds Kind Power, Nooberly will never turn it into money.

10. Changes

We may change or end the weekly pot at any time. A change starts with the next week and is posted on this page. Kind Power you already have stays yours, and it still has no cash value.

11. Who runs this

This program is run by Nooberly, Inc. Questions: team@nooberly.com.
