How every dollar flows

  1. A Tip or a Supporter Badge is bought. That is the only money that ever enters.
  2. Apple or Google take their store cut. Nobody else takes anything.
  3. The servant's declared keep, if any, comes off next — it is printed on this page.
  4. Everything left becomes the week's pot of Sooper Power (SP*), paid out in full every Friday to the members who earned the Kingdom's trust that week.
